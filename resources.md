# Other resources worth your time

This repo is deliberately narrow — three focused files, not an encyclopedia. For depth beyond that, these are still genuinely good and actively maintained as of 2026:

**System design**
- [ashishps1/awesome-system-design-resources](https://github.com/ashishps1/awesome-system-design-resources) — the most actively maintained curated list of system design material on GitHub, organized by topic (concepts, interview problems, case studies).
- [donnemartin/system-design-primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) — still the most-referenced starting point for the fundamentals (scaling, replication, caching) in plain English. Treat it as the foundation; layer this repo's 2026 rubric (cost/failure-mode reasoning, AI infra) on top of it.

**General interview prep (coding + behavioral)**
- [yangshun/tech-interview-handbook](https://github.com/yangshun/tech-interview-handbook) — comprehensive, well-curated prep material for busy engineers, including a solid behavioral-question treatment.
- [kdn251/interviews](https://github.com/kdn251/interviews) — a long-standing, widely trusted (30k+ stars) personal interview guide covering core data structures and algorithms with video walkthroughs.

If you find something better or more current, open a PR.
