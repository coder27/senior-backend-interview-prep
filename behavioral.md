# Behavioral questions

28 questions across 7 categories senior candidates get asked repeatedly. Prep a real STAR-structured story for each category ahead of time — not a memorized script, but a story you can adapt on the spot.

## Rubric

- **1** — No strong story comes to mind for this category.
- **2** — Have a relevant story, but it's vague or lacks a clear structure.
- **3** — Can tell a clear STAR-structured story, but the impact or outcome is fuzzy.
- **4** — Clear STAR story with measurable impact, appropriately scoped to a senior IC's ownership.
- **5** — Compelling story demonstrating senior-level scope, with clear reflection and lessons learned.

## Questions


### Ownership

- Tell me about a time you owned a project end-to-end with ambiguous requirements.
- Describe a time you identified and fixed a problem nobody had asked you to fix.
- Tell me about a decision you made that, looking back, you'd make differently.
- Describe a time you pushed back on a deadline you believed was unrealistic.

### Dealing with Ambiguity

- Tell me about a time you had to start work without complete requirements.
- Describe a project where the goalposts moved significantly midway through.
- Tell me about a time you had to make a consequential decision with incomplete data.
- Describe how you scoped a project when the initial ask was vague.

### Conflict & Disagreement

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a technical decision made by your manager or a senior engineer.
- Describe a conflict with a peer and how you resolved it.
- Tell me about a time you had to say no to a stakeholder.
- Describe a time your code review feedback was strongly contested.

### Failure & Learning

- Tell me about your biggest technical failure and what you learned from it.
- Describe a time a project you led missed its deadline.
- Tell me about a production incident you caused and how you handled it.
- Describe a time you gave an estimate or advice that turned out to be wrong.

### Leadership & Influence

- Tell me about a time you influenced a team's direction without formal authority.
- Describe how you mentored a junior engineer through a difficult period.
- Tell me about a time you drove a cross-team technical decision.
- Describe a time you had to rally a team around an unpopular decision.

### Cross-team Collaboration

- Tell me about a time you worked with a team whose priorities conflicted with yours.
- Describe a dependency on another team that put your project at risk.
- Tell me about a time you had to align two teams with different technical approaches.
- Describe how you handled a partner team missing a commitment you depended on.

### Prioritization & Trade-offs

- Tell me about a time you had to cut scope to hit a deadline.
- Describe how you decided between two competing technical approaches.
- Tell me about a time you said no to a feature request, and how you handled it.
- Describe a time you had to balance paying down tech debt against shipping new features.
